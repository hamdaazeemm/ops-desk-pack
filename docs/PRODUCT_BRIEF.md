# Product brief — Pentland Ops Desk

## The problem

`ops@pentlandinfra.com` receives requests, but nothing turns them into owned work. An item belongs to whoever replies first. The tracker holds about 60% of what the desk does (Ayesha). Nobody can say what is outstanding without scrolling the mailbox for ninety minutes and getting it wrong (desk notes). When the Managing Director asked what else was sitting in the inbox, "nobody could tell me. Not approximately. At all." (Tariq)

This is **a visibility and ownership problem, not a speed problem.** Most replies are already fast; Omar clears the easy mail before 09:00. What fails is everything that is not easy: it has no owner, no honest status, and no clock.

> "I did not ask for faster. I asked for visible." — Tariq

## What the pack shows the failures actually are

1. **A message is not a unit of work.** msg-004 is three asks; the risky one is the one that "disappears" (Junaid). Two asks arrive twice from two directions (004-1 with 008; 004-3 with 003). One thread changes subject into an oil leak (msg-014 inside the gate-pass thread `thr-008`).
2. **Decided is treated as done.** msg-019 "Approved on our side. Closing this one out on the tracker." The contractor in msg-017 asked for *written* approval. This is the January failure happening again today.
3. **Ownership is implicit and invisible.** "Nothing is owned until somebody replies" (desk notes). The regulator letter went through five people asking "is this yours?" (msg-010).
4. **Forwarding resets the clock.** msg-010 arrives looking new on 12 March; the notice is dated 27 February and is due on 13 March.
5. **The slowness on four categories is the only control, and it is accidental** (Elena). msg-006, 026, 028, 030.

## The bet

A work list behind the shared mailbox. A model turns each message into **asks**: it splits bundles, merges the same ask arriving twice, files noise, and suggests an owner. People keep replying in Outlook, and a reply claims the ask. An ask is resolved only when the person waiting has been told. Four categories cannot be released until their prerequisite is recorded.

The model makes the list cheap to keep. Rules and people decide anything with consequences.

## What we are not solving

- **Replying faster.** Not the stated problem, and the one Tariq said he would cut first.
- **Replacing Outlook.** Replies stay in Outlook. The product never sends email.
- **Automatic assignment.** It suggests; a person claims (Ayesha: an ignored assignment is "worse than unowned").
- **Deciding the business question.** Whether to pay Delta, approve VO-14, or bind the marine renewal stays with the people who own those decisions.
- **Reading attachment contents.** The pack gives filenames only; msg-031 is therefore an honest "needs a look".
- **Department inboxes.** The desk is five people with no rota. Hiding mail by team recreates the forwarding loop in msg-010.
- **Analytics.** "I do not need a dashboard with twelve charts" (Tariq).
