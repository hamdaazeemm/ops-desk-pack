## What the desk notes say that the mailbox does not support

- **Volume.** The notes say ~120 messages on a normal weekday. The pack README calls this 36-message Thursday "typical" volume. Omar says he clears "thirty, thirty-five" before 09:00; on this day 10 messages had arrived by 09:00. I treat 120/day as the planning figure (the brief uses it too) and cost both, but I do not trust it. See `DECISIONS.md` D15.
- **Noise share.** The notes say this day is ~53% noise (19 messages). I count 15 (42%). 19 is only reachable by also counting the redirects and the 034 closure as noise, which is the expensive mistake this product is meant to avoid.
- **"Anything from a regulator is meant to be flagged to Compliance the same day."** In 010 the letter arrived *in* the Compliance inbox and Compliance forwarded it *out*. The rule as written is not what happens.
