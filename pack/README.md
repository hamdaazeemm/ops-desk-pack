# ops-desk-pack

Provided material for the Shared-Inbox Triage take-home assignment.

Everything here is fictional. Names, companies, email addresses, invoice numbers, licence numbers and CNIC fragments are invented. Any resemblance to a real organisation is coincidental.

## Contents

| Path | What it is |
|---|---|
| `inbox.json` | One working day of the shared operations mailbox — 36 messages, in the order they arrived. |
| `how-the-desk-runs.md` | Notes from the Operations Desk Lead on how the desk works today. Written in one sitting and not revised. |
| `conversations/` | Five stakeholder conversations, lightly edited transcripts. |

## Notes on the mailbox export

- Messages are in chronological order. `thread_id` groups messages that belong to the same conversation; a thread may contain messages that arrived hours apart.
- Subject lines are as sent. They have not been corrected.
- Attachments are listed by filename only. The files themselves are not included — assume you can see the filename and nothing else.
- CNIC numbers are partially masked, as they would be in a real export.
- The day sampled is a Thursday. Volume is typical; the mix is slightly lighter on non-actionable mail than a normal day.

## Notes on the conversations

The five people do not agree with each other, and in two cases they contradict each other directly. That is not an error in the pack.

The desk notes also contain at least one statement that the mailbox does not support. Deciding what to trust is part of the assignment.
