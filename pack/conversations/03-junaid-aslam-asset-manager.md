# Conversation 3 — Junaid Aslam, Asset Manager (Thermal & Solar)

Internal requester. The desk's most frequent correspondent and its most frequent chaser. Fictional transcript, lightly edited.

---

**How often are you writing to the desk?**

Two or three times a day. I am the one on site calls, so anything the site needs from the centre, it comes through me.

**Farah says you chase a lot.**

I chase because I cannot see anything. If I send three things on Monday, by Wednesday I have no idea whether any of them have been looked at. So I send a "any update?" and that is now a fourth message on their desk, which I know is not helping. I am aware I am part of the problem. I just do not have another option.

**What would stop you chasing?**

Seeing the status. Genuinely, that is the whole thing. If I could look at a screen and see "site access — with Ayesha, expect Thursday" and "invoice — with Finance since the 3rd", I would never send a chaser again. I do not need it done fast. I need to know where it is so I can tell the person who is asking me.

**You often send several requests in one email.**

Yes, and I know they would rather I did not. But I am usually typing between site calls and I am not going to write three separate emails and three separate subject lines. If there is a system that makes me raise them one at a time, I will keep emailing.

**What happens to a bundled email now?**

Usually one of the three gets dealt with. Whichever is easiest. The other two just evaporate. Nobody says no to them, they simply do not happen, and I find out two weeks later when the site asks me.

**Which of the three is usually the one that gets done?**

The gate pass. Because it is quick and it is obvious. The insurance certificate question, which is the one that actually carries risk, is the one that disappears — because answering it means going and finding something out.

**Tell me about the Delta Civil invoice.**

That is the sort of thing that makes me look incompetent. A contractor rings me directly, asking why they have not been paid for work they did in February. I have nothing to tell them. I email the desk, the desk presumably emails Finance, Finance presumably has a reason. Somewhere in that chain there is an answer and I am the only person in the conversation who cannot see it.

And the desk is not being slow out of malice. It has gone somewhere and nobody knows where.

**Would you use a separate system, or do you want it in email?**

I will use whatever, as long as I can see status in it. If it is a website I have to log into, I will check it when I am chasing rather than logging in daily. If it emails me when something changes, better.

**What would you not accept?**

Being made to categorise my own requests. I do not know your categories and I will get them wrong, and then my request sits in the wrong pile and I am back to chasing. Let me write what I need in plain language.

**One more. If the desk had to be slower on some things in exchange for being clearer, would you take it?**

Yes, easily. Slow and visible beats fast and invisible. The problem is never that they are slow. The problem is that I am guessing.
