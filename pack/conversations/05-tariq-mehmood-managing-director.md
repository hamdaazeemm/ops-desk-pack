# Conversation 5 — Tariq Mehmood, Managing Director

Sponsor of the work. Does not use the desk himself, except to send things to it. Fictional transcript, lightly edited.

---

**What made you ask for this?**

The Zenith standing time. Nine days of plant sitting on a site because an approval we had already given was never communicated. That is not a large number in the scheme of things, but it is an embarrassing one, because nobody did anything wrong. Everyone followed the process. The process simply had a hole where "tell the contractor" should have been.

And then I asked what else was sitting in that inbox, and nobody could tell me. Not approximately. At all.

**What do you want to be able to see?**

What is outstanding, how old it is, and what is about to cost us money. That is the whole of it. I do not need a dashboard with twelve charts on it. I want to be able to look once a week and know whether anything is rotting.

**How would you know it had worked?**

If I stop hearing about things like the variation. And if, when I ask a question about the desk, the answer arrives in the time it takes to look at a screen rather than the time it takes someone to scroll a mailbox for an afternoon.

**How much appetite is there for spend?**

Modest. This is an internal desk of five people, not a product we sell. I will fund something small that works. I will not fund a programme.

If there is a model in it and the model costs money per message, I want to know what that is per month before we start, not after. And I would want to know what happens to that number if the volume doubles, because the portfolio is growing and the desk is not.

**What about the data?**

That inbox has live transaction correspondence in it, counterparty information, our banking arrangements. I am not comfortable with all of that sitting on somebody else's service being used to train something. Whatever is built, I want to be able to say clearly where the content goes and who retains it. That is a hard line rather than a preference.

**The desk team have different views on how much the system should decide for them.**

I would expect that. My view is that I do not particularly care whether a machine or a person sorts the post, as long as somebody is accountable for what happened. The thing I will not accept is a system that quietly does something wrong and nobody notices for a month. If it is going to be wrong, it should be wrong loudly.

**One of the coordinators says any extra step will kill adoption.**

That is Omar, and he is right about himself. He is also the reason the last two attempts failed, and I am aware that we are about to attempt a third.

What I would say is that I am not prepared to let one person's preference set the ceiling on how this company operates. If the answer is that the tool has to be invisible to him and useful to everyone else, that is a legitimate answer and somebody should design for it. But "Omar will not use it" is a constraint to be solved, not a veto.

**Is there anything you would cut first if time ran out?**

Anything that makes the desk faster. I did not ask for faster. I asked for visible.
